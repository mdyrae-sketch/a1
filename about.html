Our Story

Built for students who finish courses

We're a small, independent course studio — not a large platform. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

Too many all-nighters that didn't actually work

Brain Study Map started after noticing the same pattern across years of studying: hours of re-reading notes the night before an exam that still didn't stick the next morning. We started building courses around visual mapping and spaced review instead of passive re-reading.

Every course since has been tested with real students against real course material before it's published.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every course

I.

Piloted with real students

Every course runs with a small group of real learners before it goes live.

II.

Applied to your own material

Lessons have you map your actual subject, not a generic example.

III.

Short by design

We cut course length aggressively rather than pad it to look substantial.
