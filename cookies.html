Cookie & Tracking Technology Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 • Version 2.8 • Transparency & Consent

This Cookie Policy explains how SpiceBowlMint Botanical Atelier LLC ("SpiceBowlMint", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies, pixel tags, local storage objects, and related tracking technologies when you navigate spicebowlmint.com. We believe in total transparency regarding how technology interacts with your browser.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your computer, tablet, or smartphone when you visit a digital page. Cookies enable web servers to remember your actions and preferences (such as display theme, language, and navigation flow) over a period of time, eliminating the need to re-enter settings whenever you return to the site.

Local Storage (HTML5 localStorage): In addition to traditional cookies, we utilize modern browser local storage. Unlike cookies, local storage data is not transmitted to web servers with every HTTP request; it resides securely within your local browser sandbox to provide lightning-fast UI state restoration.

2. Categories of Cookies We Utilize

We categorize the tracking technologies on our website into three distinct functional tiers:

Cookie Category Provider / Tool Primary Purpose Storage Duration
Essential / Functional SpiceBowlMint (First-Party) Stores visual theme preference (sbm_theme: Light/Dark) and mobile drawer states. Persistent (localStorage)
Performance & Analytics Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_*) Collects pseudonymized metrics on page visit sequences, scroll depth, and monograph reading time. 2 years / 14 months
Interactive Tools SpiceBowlMint (First-Party) Temporarily caches custom grain bowl selections within the Bowl Architect session. Session Only

3. Detailed Explanation of Analytics Cookies

We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Measurement Tag: G-0LY0HY7L01) to understand how visitors discover and engage with our culinary content. Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish unique users and throttle request rates. We have configured Google Analytics with:

  • IP Anonymization: IP addresses are truncated before processing to protect geographic anonymity.
  • Disabled Advertising Features: We do not share analytics data with Google advertising networks or third-party retargeting pixels.
  • Restricted Data Retention: User-level event records automatically expire after 14 months.

4. Managing and Controlling Your Cookie Preferences

You have complete control over how cookies and local storage are utilized on your device:

A. Browser-Level Controls

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage cookie settings through their preferences menu. You can configure your browser to block third-party cookies, alert you when a cookie is placed, or delete all stored cookies upon closing your browser window. Please consult your browser's documentation (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) for specific instructions.

B. Google Analytics Opt-Out

To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites, you can download and install the official Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

5. Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect additions to our interactive culinary features or shifts in data privacy legislation. Any modifications will take effect immediately upon posting to this page.
