Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 • Last Revised: September 6, 2026 • Version 3.4

At SpiceBowlMint Botanical Atelier LLC ("SpiceBowlMint", "we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to upholding the highest standards of data stewardship, digital privacy, and transparency. This comprehensive Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, utilize, secure, and disclose information when you access our website located at spicebowlmint.com, engage with our interactive Spice & Mint Bowl Architect tool, subscribe to our culinary monographs, or communicate with our culinary consultation desk.

1. Foundational Commitment to Digital Privacy

Just as we value the purity and integrity of whole botanical ingredients in our kitchen, we value the sanctity and confidentiality of your personal information. We operate under strict principles of data minimization—collecting only the essential data required to provide you with rich culinary education, recipe customization, and seamless user experiences. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or behavioral advertising networks under any circumstances.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website and digital services, we may collect several distinct categories of information:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide to Us

  • Contact and Identity Information: When you submit an inquiry through our Atelier Desk, request a private consultation, or subscribe to our botanical journal, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and the textual content of your message.
  • Culinary Preferences and Recipe Configurations: When you utilize our interactive Spice & Mint Bowl Architect, we process your selections (such as preferred grain foundations, mint varietals, and whole spice tarka blends) to render real-time flavor notes. These preferences are stored locally on your device unless you choose to email or save the recipe card.
  • Survey and Feedback Data: If you participate in our seasonal culinary surveys, recipe feedback forms, or masterclass evaluations, we record your responses to improve our educational publications and kitchen formulations.

B. Information Collected Automatically Through Digital Technologies

  • Device and Telemetry Data: When you browse our digital pages, our servers automatically log technical attributes including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, device screen resolution, language settings, and referring Uniform Resource Locators (URLs).
  • Usage and Interaction Metrics: We record aggregate metrics regarding how visitors navigate our pages, including time spent reading specific spice monographs, click sequences, scrolling depth, and tool engagement patterns via Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01).
  • Local Browser Storage Data: We utilize browser local storage (localStorage) exclusively to remember your visual aesthetic theme preference (Fresh Garden Mint Light vs. Twilight Herbal Dark) so that your chosen interface persists across visits without requiring login credentials.

3. Lawful Bases and Purposes for Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly in accordance with applicable global data protection frameworks (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation [GDPR] and the California Consumer Privacy Act [CCPA/CPRA]). Our processing activities rely on the following lawful bases:

  1. Performance of a Contractual or Service Request: To respond to your culinary inquiries, deliver requested recipe cards, manage masterclass bookings, and provide educational newsletter subscriptions.
  2. Legitimate Business Interests: To maintain the security, uptime, and performance of our web infrastructure; prevent malicious bot activity; debug technical anomalies; and refine the visual design of our interactive botanical tools.
  3. Compliance with Legal Obligations: To adhere to applicable statutory recordkeeping, tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements.
  4. Consent: Where you have granted explicit consent, such as opting into our monthly botanical monographs or permitting non-essential analytics tracking. You retain the right to withdraw consent at any time.

4. Analytics, Cookies, and Tracking Technologies

Our website employs Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, pseudonymized information about visitor traffic and interaction patterns. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to track interaction sequences. IP addresses are anonymized before storage, and we have disabled Google signals and demographic data sharing. You can prevent Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on or adjusting your cookie preferences via our Cookie Policy.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not disclose your personal data to external entities, except in the following limited, legally regulated scenarios:

  • Trusted Technical Service Providers: We engage reputable cloud hosting providers, email delivery platforms, and infrastructure vendors who process data strictly under our written instructions and binding confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal and Regulatory Compliance: We may disclose information if required by enforceable subpoena, court order, or applicable law to protect the legal rights, safety, and security of SpiceBowlMint, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of atelier assets, customer data may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to the recipient assuming identical privacy obligations.

6. International Data Transfers

SpiceBowlMint operates its primary servers and culinary atelier in the United States. If you access our website from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other jurisdictions with international transfer regulations, please note that your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. We implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and robust technical safeguards to ensure that your personal information receives equivalent protection regardless of geography.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Schedules

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the specific purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Inquiry and Consultation Records: Retained for 24 months following resolution to facilitate ongoing culinary consultation and historical correspondence.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Retained until you click the "Unsubscribe" link included in every email or submit a deletion request.
  • Aggregated Web Analytics Logs: Retained within Google Analytics for a maximum of 14 months before automated permanent deletion.

8. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Depending on your place of residence, you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a machine-readable copy of the personal data we hold concerning you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your personal data from our active databases.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to data processing based on legitimate interests or request restrictions on specific processing activities.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, alter pricing, or degrade your user experience for exercising any of your statutory privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@spicebowlmint.com or via postal mail at 340 S Lemon Ave, Walnut, CA 91789, United States. We respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) calendar days.

9. Security Measures and Technical Safeguards

We implement multi-layered physical, technical, and organizational security protocols to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All web traffic is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Our server environments undergo periodic vulnerability scans, and access to internal databases is restricted to authorized personnel under strict principle-of-least-privilege controls.

10. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our culinary services, emerging technologies, or evolving legal requirements. When modifications occur, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this document regularly to stay informed regarding our data protection practices.
